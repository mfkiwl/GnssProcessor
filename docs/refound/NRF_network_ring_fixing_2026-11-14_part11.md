## 90. 2026-11-17: the continuity guard in the network: built behind a key and validated against its offline twin

**Why** (sections 83.1, 83.2, 83.4 and 89; the author's instruction of the night to implement where needed): flagged levels move within runs of constant counters on both 24 h days (DOY 146 E10, E11, E25 by 1.4 to 1.6 cycles; DOY 147 E29 by 2.1), and section 89 tests offline what a guard does for the users. This section builds the guard into the network so that it can be armed by a key once the users' test decides.

**The build** (this commit; default off and byte-identical off):
- Keys `network.ring_fix.continuity_guard_cyc` (0 = off; in (0, 1]; needs `network.ring_fix.unsettled_steps`), `network.ring_fix.continuity_guard_hold` (20 emissions) and `network.ring_fix.continuity_guard_min_run` (4); ledger rows UNVALIDATED, PARAM, PARAM.
- `NetworkProductEmitter`: per held family and satellite with both phase signals, both code biases of the same bands and a clock, the level of section 89 in narrow-lane cycles, less the family's running datum (the median epoch-to-epoch step over 4 or more satellites at both emissions); a run of emissions with both flags up and no step of any rule; after the minimum run, a level more than the threshold from its run's start trips the guard: both flags lowered at that emission and the next hold − 1, both counters stepped at the trip, the unsettled steps stepping them while lowered and once more when the flags rise. `applyContinuityGuardFlags` lowers the same flags in the solution record after the emission, because the product file is written from it (`network/emit/EmitStore.cpp`). Not carried across a warm resume.
- The arming line at setup and a run-end report (trips per family, key-emissions lowered).
- Suite `ContinuityGuard` (`tests/test_continuity_guard.cpp`, 6 cases): arming needs the unsettled steps and legal values; a static family never trips; a moving flagged level (1 mm of phase bias per epoch, 0.0208 cycle) trips at the 0.15 bar, lowers both flags in the store and in the solution, holds them for the hold with the counters stepping and settles once; an unflagged satellite is not guarded; a step of another rule restarts the run; off is untouched. The 6 cases pass on the Debug build, with the neighbouring suites (269 cases in 74 suites: the unsettled steps, the loss-of-lock mask, the emitter, the configuration loader, the ledger and posture checks; log `_irdu_grades/test_cg_debug.log`).

**Validation** (exe nrf35 `61cdf1a085d8ac58`; `cgA2_n144`: `f13_n144`'s configuration for 3 h with the guard off, `cgB2_n144`: the same with `continuity_guard_cyc` 0.15; a 10-epoch smoke first, exit 0, the arming line printed; script `compare_guard.py`, grade `_irdu_grades/nrf_cg_validate2.txt`):
- Off against on: 0 records differ outside the P records' flag and counter fields: the guard does not touch the solution.
- On: 15 trips (Galileo C/Q 6, X/X 5, GPS 4), 586 key-emissions lowered; the offline twin (`stream_guard.py --mode unsettled` on `cgA2_n144`) gives the same 15 trips and 0 P records differing from `cgB2_n144`: the in-network guard is the offline guard, record for record.
- Off against the adopted configuration's run: `cgA2_n144`'s stream equals `f13_n144`'s (exe nrf28) byte for byte over its 183 149 lines; the guard and every key built tonight leave the adopted configuration untouched.
- The first build (nrf34 `fc31de315517026a`) lowered the flags in the store and not in the file: the same comparison showed 586 P records with the flag up and the counters stepping; the solution-record step was added and the comparison rerun.

**Reading.** The guard exists in the network, default off, and emits exactly what section 89's offline guard writes, so the users' test of section 89 is its test too: bars passing there make it a candidate for the chain posture (a ledger row and a posture row), bars failing leave it off.

## 92. 2026-11-17: the Galileo C/Q family rooted at a mid-latitude receiver on DOY 147 (pre-registered)

**Why** (sections 83.1 and 83.4): on both 24 h days the Galileo L1C/L5Q family's held share falls through the middle of the day: DOY 146 from SGOC00LKA, 61, 58, 53, 48, 44 % in hours 10 to 14 and 27 % in hour 20; DOY 147 from DJIG00DJI, 64, 56, 54, 47, 46 % in hours 10 to 14, recovering from hour 19. The same DOY 146 rooted at WTZR00DEU (`d24r_n146`, the second run of the two-run audit) falls much less: 77, 74, 74, 74, 73 % in hours 10 to 14, lowest 56 % in hour 18. Both elected roots lie under the equatorial ionosphere (Sri Lanka, Djibouti), as does MBAR00UGA, whose evening disturbance carries DOY 146's Galileo X/X failure as that family's root. The election takes the configured receiver, else the satellites' sticky vote, else the remembered root, else the receiver with the most satellites (`electDatumReceiver`, `network/solve/ChainDatum.cpp`), and a count of satellites favours low latitudes. The hypothesis: a root under a disturbed ionosphere carries its own errors into its family's levels. The test repeats DOY 146's comparison on the second day.

**The arm** (exe nrf32 `cf0816d6d26139ea`, the exe of `d24_n147`; `scripts/rapid_arm.py make`): `d24w_n147` is `d24_n147`'s configuration (base `_irdu_grades/base_n147_24h.json` with `processing.exact_epoch_time` true) with `network.phase_datum_eq34_receiver` WTZR00DEU, for 14 h from the day's start (hours 10 to 14 hold the decline to its lowest hour). WTZR00DEU tracks Galileo L1C/L5Q and GPS, so the key roots those two families there (GPS from BOR100POL in `d24_n147`); the Galileo X/X family keeps its election (BOGT00COL in `d24_n147`). A 10-epoch smoke first.

**Bars** (against `d24_n147` over hours 1 to 14, `d24_hourly.py` as in section 83.4):
1. The hypothesis holds on the second day if the Galileo C/Q held share from WTZR00DEU is at least 65 % in each of hours 10 to 14.
2. It fails if that share falls within 5 points of `d24_n147`'s in at least 3 of hours 10 to 14.
3. Conditions of the run: every epoch emitted; GPS held at least 80 % in every hour from 3; WTZR00DEU the root of both C/Q bands all run. Reported, not a bar: Galileo X/X by hour (on DOY 146 the X/X family, with the same root in both runs, held 4 to 13 points less in hours 2 to 9 when the other families were rooted at WTZR00DEU), and the C/Q unsettled share of satellite-hours.

**Reading prepared.** Bar 1 passing on a second day makes the root a lever of the family's daytime levels, and an election rule the candidate for the author (a quiet receiver preferred by a slip-rate or ionosphere-rate score, or a band of geomagnetic latitude), with its own A/B over a day and the cost of a root change in the stream. Bar 2 passing says DOY 146's difference was that day's or SGOC00LKA's alone, and the daytime decline is the family's own.
