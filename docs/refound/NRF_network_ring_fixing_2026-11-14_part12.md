## 96. 2026-11-17: the pieces of the Galileo C/Q held network on DOY 146 and 147 (a read)

**Why** (the requesting session's message, item C2): the requesting session reads the C/Q family's lost satellites as a network too thin to stay in one piece (with section 88.1: the same satellites stay flagged in X/X and come back to their offset). Asked: the connected pieces of receivers and satellites through held links by hour, which piece holds the root, in which piece the lost satellites sit, and whether the links to them are integer-consistent inside their piece.

**The graph** is the settled rule's own (`network.ring_fix.settled_rule` "largest", `ringFixLargestSettledSet` in `network/ar/RingFix.hpp`): receivers and satellites joined by the family's held links (`network_ring_fix.csv`) and its certified tree edges (`network_chain_edges.csv`). The stream flags the satellites of the largest piece (at least 4 satellites, a lead of 2 to replace the incumbent, a wait of 10 emissions on the raise), whatever receiver roots it. No tree edge of either Galileo family is certified on either day (`cq_tree_cert.py`), so the graph is the held links alone. Sampled every 30 min from hour 1, 46 samples per day (`cq_pieces.py`, `cq_lost_links.py`, `cq_tree_cert.py` in `_irdu_grades/scripts_s20/`; grades in `_irdu_grades/grades_family_if/`); the epochs of DOY 146, written without the exact epoch time, are matched to the nearest second.

**Results.**
- *Pieces.* DOY 147: one piece of 13 to 25 satellites and 14 to 26 receivers at every sample, flagged; a second piece of 2 to 4 satellites at 4 of the 46 samples. DOY 146: one piece of 18 to 26 satellites to hour 12, then of 5 to 18, with second and third pieces of 2 to 6 satellites (1 to 8 receivers) at 13 of the 24 samples from hour 12; at those samples the stream's flagged satellites fall in two or three pieces of the graph read here, which the network may join through links this read does not see. Adding the second band's certified edges or the links decided at the epoch changes nothing beyond one satellite-sample.
- *The root's piece.* The datum receiver (SGOC00LKA on DOY 146, DJIG00DJI on DOY 147) is in the flagged piece at 33 and 25 of the 46 samples; at the others it holds no link of the family.
- *The lost satellites* (tree satellites not flagged): 437 satellite-samples on DOY 146 and 390 on DOY 147; 435 and 384 of them hold no link at all, the rest are in the flagged piece but not yet flagged, or alone with one receiver; none sits in a second piece of 2 or more satellites. The flagged satellites hold 3.5 and 3.9 links on average. The X/X family loses 58 and 25 satellite-samples on the same days, its flagged satellites holding 4.0 and 4.3 links.
- *Their links* (the candidate links of a lost satellite where it has at least 3; median 8 on DOY 146, 9 on DOY 147; refused by fraction, odds and window): the fractions of their floats in the chain frame agree with each other, the ionosphere-free fraction to a spread of 0.07 and 0.04 cycle and the wide lane to 0.08 and 0.07, around a common offset from the piece's integers of 0.22 and 0.17 cycle (ionosphere-free) and 0.20 and 0.24 cycle (wide lane). The refused links of flagged satellites spread 0.11 and 0.08 (ionosphere-free) and 0.15 and 0.14 (wide lane).
- *One satellite's offset over time.* E36 on DOY 147 rests on CEBR00ESP from 10.0 to 16.5 h with 8 to 12 links whose common offset wanders from +0.04 to +0.46 cycle (ionosphere-free, spreads 0.02 to 0.09) and through the whole wide-lane cycle (spreads 0.05 to 0.16); at 17.0 h its tree edge moves to DJIG00DJI, the offset falls to +0.02 and +0.03, and the stream flags it again from 17.5 h. E04 and E25 keep offsets of 0.3 to 0.4 wide-lane cycle with their tree edges at DJIG00DJI itself, so the tree edge's receiver is not the whole story.

**Reading.** The C/Q family does not break into pieces that carry their own satellites: its held network is one piece at nearly every sample, the root is often outside it (rule "largest" flags the piece whatever roots it), and the lost satellites are outside every piece, without a single held link. Their links are integer-consistent with each other but not with the piece: each lost satellite's level, as the C/Q network carries it, is off the piece's integers by a common fraction that wanders over hours, so every one of its links is refused against it. The satellite is fine in the requesting session's sense (its links agree, and X/X holds it with the same ionosphere-free offset when C/Q takes it back, 88.1); the defect is a satellite level that no held link ties, rather than a split network. Section 92.2's "term carried by the satellites" is read accordingly: a term of the C/Q network's own level for those satellites, not of their signal. The thinness enters as satellites resting on few links (section 97).

## 97. 2026-11-17: E30 on DOY 147 from 5.5 to 9.0 h: the C/Q family's level moves, on one held link and then none (a trace)

**Why** (item C3): E30's ionosphere-free offset between the families grows from +0.02 at 5.5 h to +0.33 at 8.5 h with both families flagged (88.1). Asked: which family's level moves, and on how many held links each family carries E30 in those hours.

**Trace** (`sat_family_series.py`, every 5 min from 5.0 to 9.5 h; `_irdu_grades/grades_family_if/e30_d24_n147.txt`): per family, E30's ionosphere-free phase bias (narrow-lane cycles) and wide-lane phase bias (cycles) in the stream about their values at 5.0 h, its held links (`network_ring_fix.csv`) and its tree edge (`network_chain_edges.csv`).
- *C/Q:* held links 2 to 5.17 h, 1 from 5.25 to 7.67 h, none from 7.75 h; tree edge at MAC100AUS, at SOLO00SLB from 5.50 h, MAC100AUS from 5.92 h, SOLO00SLB from 7.08 h and JPLM00USA from 8.67 h; flagged to 8.58 h. The wide-lane bias is −0.03 cycle at 5.5 h, −0.11 at 6.5, −0.21 at 7.0, −0.36 at 7.5, −0.42 at 8.0 and −0.53 at 8.5 h. At 8.67 h, when the tree edge moves to JPLM00USA and the flags drop, the level steps by −1.55 narrow-lane cycles (ionosphere-free) and −0.40 cycle (wide lane).
- *X/X:* held links 1 to 4 (2 at most samples) throughout; tree edge at SCTB00ATA, at GAMB00PYF from 5.25 h; flagged throughout. Its wide-lane bias stays within +0.002 and +0.028 cycle of its 5.0 h value.
- *Both:* the ionosphere-free biases drift by −0.39 (C/Q) and −0.25 cycle (X/X) by 8.5 h, as the other satellites' do (their median absolute change 0.10 to 0.16 cycle from 7 to 8.5 h in both families), a drift common to the satellites; between the families E30 moves by −0.14 cycle, in C/Q.

**Reading.** The C/Q family's E30 moves, not the X/X one: half a wide-lane cycle in three hours while it rests on one held link and then on its tree edge alone, with its flags up (a satellite in the largest piece stays flagged on a single link), while X/X carries it on two links with its wide lane steady to 0.03 cycle. A satellite level carried by one link takes that link's errors (here at SOLO00SLB and MAC100AUS) and the flags do not see it: section 96's defect caught before the satellite leaves the piece. A candidate, not built: a satellite's flag lowered while it rests on fewer than two held links (E30 from 5.25 h), with its cost in held share measured first.

## 98. 2026-11-17: design note: one Galileo network per band (no code; bars pre-registered for a later build)

**Why** (item C4; sections 88, 88.1, 96 and 97): the two Galileo families are separate networks on the same satellites. They share one satellite level in the ionosphere-free direction (to 0.02 to 0.03 cycle, 88.1) and in the wide-lane direction up to a per-satellite term (up to 0.2 cycle); the C/Q family loses satellites whose level no held link ties (96) while X/X holds them, and a C/Q level resting on one link drifts (97). One network per band lets the held links of either family tie the other's satellite levels. Not built: no build until the author decides (the requesting session's order).

**The design**, along section 88's points:
1. *The satellite level, one per band.* One satellite phase-bias column per satellite and band (E1, E5a) for both tracking modes. In the ionosphere-free direction the modes share it to 0.02 to 0.03 cycle; the constant between the families (their datum difference) goes to one family's receiver phase biases, as an inter-signal constant does today (section 88 point 5). The ionosphere direction (0.10 to 0.18 cycle per band) is the slant-ionosphere level each family's code sets; the free per-LOS ionosphere keeps it in the links' states, with no state of its own.
2. *A per-satellite wide-lane term between the modes.* The wide-lane integer of a link is set through the code (the Melbourne-Wübbena direction), so each family's code level per satellite enters its wide lane: 88.1 measures their difference per satellite at up to 0.2 cycle (DOY 147: E12 −0.225, E32 +0.184), constant within a day and different between days. One state per satellite (the combined mode's code level against the pilot's in that direction), estimable where receivers of both modes see the satellite, born from the first epochs at which both modes hold it, without process noise within a day and with a slip and re-seed response (standing invariant 1: a jump in the term is an event at both edges, with counters). Without it a tie puts up to 0.2 wide-lane cycle into every chord that joins the modes.
3. *One tree per band* in the chain: `formChainFamilies` groups by band, the pilot and combined signals of a band in one group, the datum receiver elected over both modes' receivers (section 88 point 2).
4. *Ring-fix families by band:* E1/E5a as one family, so a chord may join a pilot link and a combined link, its wide lane an integer once point 2's term is applied.
5. *The stream:* one phase bias per satellite and band for both kinds of user and the per-satellite term for the users of the combined mode (or the per-signal biases with the tie inside), flags per band. The users' engine applies the term to a combined-mode user's wide lane (the requesting session's engine).

**The simulator first.** The simulator (`src/sim`) writes Galileo as C1C/L1C and C5Q/L5Q only (`src/sim/main.cpp`, and `SimConfig.cpp` accepts no other Galileo code), so it cannot carry two tracking modes of one band. The simulator step adds a tracking mode per station for E1 and E5a (C1X/L1X and C5X/L5X written for the combined stations), an offset between the modes in the ionosphere-free direction (one constant) and in the wide-lane direction (per satellite, up to 0.2 cycle, constant within a day), and the receiver-side offsets the observed data show. Its truth grades the tie before any observed day: the shared level recovered to the injected constant, the per-satellite term within its formal sigma, no wrong integer in the chords that join the modes.

**Bars, pre-registered for DOY 146 and 147** (24 h each, against `d24_n146` and `d24_n147`; the tie behind a key, default off and byte-identical off):
1. Galileo C/Q held share at least 75 % in every hour from 3 (today as low as 27 % in DOY 146's hour 20 and 46 % in DOY 147's hour 14), and its lost satellite-samples of section 96 at most a third of today's (437 and 390).
2. Galileo X/X and GPS held shares not lowered by more than 2 points in any hour from 3.
3. The two-run audit (two datum receivers, held states): no more failing checks than the same audit of today's runs in any family, and every failing check in a chord that joins the modes read in the post-fit residuals (section 83.5's test).
4. The users at the seven receivers of the second Galileo family (whole day, fixing and float): no hold wrong by the float check, near-null wide-lane errors not above today's count, Galileo reference changes fewer than 80 per run at each, and their accuracy (median of the hourly 3D median) not worse by more than 1 mm in any hour from 3.
5. Conditions: every epoch emitted; RAIM rows not above today's; the per-satellite term's hourly change at most 0.05 cycle except at a declared event.

**Reading prepared.** Bars 1 to 4 passing make the tie the C/Q family's repair and end the separate Galileo networks. Bar 1 failing with bars 2 to 4 passing says the lost satellites have a cause other than an untied level, and section 97's single-link rule comes next. The build touches the satellite column's key, the chain's grouping, the ring fix's families and the stream (section 88), and the users' engine for the combined mode's term (the requesting session's).
