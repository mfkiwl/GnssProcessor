### 13 2026-11-14: are the steps of the first 37 minutes IF-TD triggers? (part 1 from traces; part 2 pre-registered before its runs)

**Assigned** by the requesting session on the author's return. The author's words: "I recall those first ~30 minutes of jumps are from the IF TD slip detection triggers, maybe we need a better approach if this is confirmed as the problem." The finding in question is part 4's: on Q1's stream (every link at its true integer, the bed without re-seats) 1 542 declared phase steps in the first 37 minutes and none after, and the 27 resets of users starting at 30 minutes, explained in 12.11 as the network settling, with no trigger named. Part 1 attributes every declared step of the first 40 minutes on three runs, bins the slip flags by detector against the fixture's true slips, and details every IF-TD flag. Part 2: two arms of one key each on the control's executable, cold start, 2 h: (i) the IF-TD off, a measurement only (the bed has no slips, so the upper bound of a perfect detector; not a candidate setting, standing invariant 1), and (ii) the built warm-up abstain of the reference gate. If part 1 shows the IF-TD is not the trigger, (ii) is skipped, the trigger is named, and the one arm that removes it runs instead, on the same reads. Part 3 (candidate approaches, the abstain's detection-power check) applies only if the IF-TD is confirmed. Simulator only; nothing merged, pushed or armed; no reference configuration, ledger verdict or card status changed.

**(c) The IF-TD flags.** None on the bed without slips. On r3c the one flag is the reference gate's reset of S43000SIM/G22 at 15.0 min, the arc and epoch of the fixture's true slip: a true detection. In the same epoch MW refloats G22 and G19 at that station, the GF jump test marks G22 (step +4.758 m), the band scope frees both bands of nine arcs at the station, and the station's wet-delay carry is latched; the station's tree link (G15) is kept. **What the steps are.** The number of steps at an epoch follows the move every satellite shares at that epoch (Q1: 14.0 min, a shared move of -4.8 mm on L1C and 4 steps; 17.0 min, +42.0 mm and 22 steps). That shared move is the same number of cycles on both bands (the ratio of the L2W to the L1C move 1.24 to 1.31 at every epoch read, the wavelength ratio 1.283); the receivers' phase biases, the pinned root's excepted, move by the same amount the other way (26.0 min: satellites -63.6 and -80.6 mm, receivers +65.6 and +82.7 mm, the receivers' phase-bias formal sigma 3.1 m at that epoch); the median move of the satellite clocks is 0 at every epoch (the clock datum). Its median size is 0.11 cycle per epoch at 5 to 40 min on all three runs (0.113, 0.122, 0.111) and 0.021 at 40 to 120 min. The steps a satellite's own part alone would declare (its move less the shared move) are 772 of Q1's 1 542, by 5 minutes 399, 220, 99, 38, 10, 6 and none after 30 min; the shared move alone crosses the gate for 1 014. On Q1 the shared move follows the clock error of S30000SIM, the network's clock datum station and the root whose receiver phase bias is pinned (correlation of the shared move with that station's epoch-to-epoch clock error -0.91 at 5 to 40 min, -0.80 at 15 to 40, -0.73 at 40 to 120; with the median of the other stations -0.55, -0.19, -0.16). The station clock is a free column with no time link on this path (the loader's own comment at `network.carry_rx_clock_in_block`). The link is weaker on the plain chain arm (-0.36, -0.58, -0.63) and absent on r3c (-0.07, -0.19, +0.11), so it describes Q1, where every link is held, and is not the general mechanism.

**The trigger, named.** The emitter's value test (`network.disc_threshold_frac_cycles` 0.25: a move above a quarter cycle between consecutive emissions is a declared step) applied to phase biases that are still converging. Until about 37 minutes the shared move (median 0.11 cycle per epoch) plus each satellite's own convergence exceed the bar; no detector, chain event or datum event is involved after 13 minutes. The IF-TD is not the trigger on these runs, so arm (ii) is skipped as assigned: the abstain has nothing to remove (no reference-gate candidate while cold on any of the three runs, nor on the golden arm with the holds on either bed).

Arm (iii) is the arm that removes the named trigger. It removes the declarations, not the moves: the stream's values are unchanged, and a user without the resets carries the moves in its float ambiguities. The posture table flags the value as off-posture (0.24 to 0.26); the arm is a measurement, not a candidate setting.

**Reads on every arm.** Declared steps in the first 40 minutes (count, summed moves) and the time of the last declared step (`scripts/irdu_step_cause.py steps`); the stream's bars, the wide-lane and narrow-lane offsets p50 and the step p50 pooled over the pairs, in the four 15-minute bins of hour 1 and the four of hour 2 (`scripts/irdu_stream_depth.py --source prehold`); the users from 0, 15 and 30 minutes, float, the five users of `scripts/irdu_users.py` (median of the users' 3D error per 15 minutes from each user's start, `scripts/irdu_late_start.py`; the phase-bias and code-bias resets summed over the users, `scripts/irdu_user_resets.py`). The users run on Q1's arms; they run on the plain chain arm's too if its network reads differ from Q1's in direction or by more than 20 % of the control's steps. User executable: `D:/GnssProcessorSupport/gates/exe/irdu/gnss_processor_irdu_ur1.exe` (sha256[:16] `26891eb554aa3b73`, this branch at `bc643f2`; its float users are identical to `fp2`'s, 12.11), folders with the suffix `_bur1`. For the control and arm (iii) the users also run with the user set `du` (12.11's rule, `network.closed_loop.drop_unrecorded`): on this thin bed the recipe's users also use three satellites the stream does not correct, so a shared move is a between-satellite move for them (12.9), and only with the rule does arm (iii) read the declarations' cost apart from that.

**Bars.**

- Control determinism: `irdu_q1c_nr3`'s products.txt byte-identical to `_rap_irdu_q1_nr3`'s. If not, every comparison uses the re-run.
- (i) IF-TD off. Prediction from part 1 (no IF-TD flag on this bed): products byte-identical to the control's on both bases; failing that, declared steps in the first 40 minutes within 2 % in count and summed moves, the last declared step within 1 minute, every stream bar within 0.005 cycle, users within 2 mm in every bin and resets within 2 %. If the products are byte-identical, the users are not re-run (same input, same output). Held: the IF-TD is not the trigger, confirmed by experiment. Not held (steps down by more than 10 %, or the last step earlier by more than 5 minutes): the detector acts through a path other than its flags, which is then traced.
- (iii) Declarations off. Predictions: the P, C and B values of products.txt byte-identical to the control's (only the S record's discfrac token differs); 0 declared value steps (forced bumps as in the control); the users' phase-bias resets 0 apart from forced bumps. The users' float position is the measurement and no direction is claimed. Read in advance: with the rule and without it both better, the declarations cost the users; with the rule better and without it worse, the declarations protect only users that mix in uncorrected satellites; both worse, the satellites' own moves need the resets.

**Decisions in the author's absence, for review.** (1) Arm (ii) skipped by the assignment's own rule (part 1: no reference-gate candidate while cold). (2) The arm that removes the trigger is the value test out of reach (`network.disc_threshold_frac_cycles` 1000) rather than an estimator change: the trigger is the declaration of convergence moves, and the moves themselves have no single cause (the datum station's clock on Q1 only). The emission hold, which also removes the moves from the stream, is two keys and leans on the a priori clock (on the simulator, the truth's own clock); the golden arm with the holds already reads it on both beds (part 4). (3) Part 3 is not run: the IF-TD is not confirmed, so no candidate approach for it is listed and the abstain's detection-power check is not made. (4) The users run on this branch's build, with the user set `du` added for the control and arm (iii). (5) The users from 15 minutes are new folders (`_s15`); the control's users run afresh on `ur1` for all three starts, so every comparison is one executable.
